Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Who we are

"Model Diff" is a private publishing tool run by the owner of the Model Diff YouTube channel. It is used only by the channel owner, on the owner's own channel.

YouTube API Services

Model Diff uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is also covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What data we access

When the channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission, Model Diff receives:

  • an OAuth access token and refresh token for the owner's account;
  • the owner's channel ID, title and description;
  • the IDs and status of the videos, Shorts, thumbnails, subtitles and comments that the tool itself uploads or posts.

Model Diff does not access viewers' data, other channels' data, private messages, contacts, analytics of other users, or payment information.

How we use it

The data is used only to upload and publish the channel's own videos, set their metadata, post the channel's first comment under them, and update the channel's own description. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose, and it is never sold.

Storage and sharing

Tokens and channel data are stored only on the owner's private server, in files readable only by the tool. They are not shared with or transferred to any third party. Video files and scripts are the channel's own content.

Cookies and tracking

This website and the tool use no cookies, no analytics and no tracking.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while the tool is connected and deleted when the owner disconnects it. Stored channel data is deleted within 30 days of disconnection, or on request. You can revoke Model Diff's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

Any change to this policy is published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: github.com/Wildchild98/model-diff/issues
